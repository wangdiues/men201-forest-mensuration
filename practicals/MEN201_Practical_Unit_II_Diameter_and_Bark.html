Unit II
Practical
Aim. Measure one tree three ways and account for the differences. Then record a plot of trees to a stated protocol, so that a second crew could reproduce every number. DBH reference height is protocol-dependent — Bhutan's Second National Forest Inventory recorded over-bark DBH at 1.37 m; many international manuals use 1.30 m. Write down which you used.

Working tolerances

Blind re-measure — DBH 0.3 cm · girth 1.0 cm · one-sided bark 2 mm · reference-height mark 2 cm. Teaching values; real work follows the governing field manual.
