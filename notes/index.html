The notesUnits I to VII

Each unit follows the same thirteen-part structure: learning outcomes, key terminology, the major concepts, the equations and what every symbol means, step-by-step worked examples, the Bhutan-specific procedures and applications, assumptions and limitations, common errors, field-practical guidance, practice questions, a summary, and references.

  • Sessions 1–2
    Unit I
    Introduction to Forest Mensuration

    What the subject is and why anything is measured at all · the four objectives · the six reasons only relative accuracy is attainable · precision against accuracy

  • Sessions 3–4
    Unit II
    Diameter, Girth and Bark Thickness

    Girth, diameter and basal area · instruments and their error signatures · breast height and the national protocol · awkward stems · bark conversion · calibration and tolerances

  • Session 5
    Unit III
    Measurement of Crown Closure

    The four crown terms · crown closure, canopy cover and canopy closure kept apart · Bhutan's 2024 canopy procedure in full · the comparative methods · sources of error

  • Sessions 6–9
    Unit IV
    Measurement of Tree Height

    Six heights and which answers which question · the shadow and pole methods · the tangent method in four cases · the sine method in six · DBH and height classes

  • Sessions 10–12
    Unit V
    Measurement of Tree Volume

    Stem form and the form factor · Smalian, Huber and Newton · the quarter-girth convention · volume tables · the Department's Models 7 and 16 and their domain

  • Sessions 13–14
    Unit VI
    Measurement of the Crop

    Basal area three ways · crop, mean and top diameter · Lorey's height · crop age and volume · biomass and carbon · sampling, variance and confidence intervals · QA/QC

  • Sessions 15–17
    Unit VII
    Age and Growth of Trees

    Five ways to age a tree · the four classes of increment · culmination of the m.a.i and what it does not decide · increment percentage by three formulae

The handbookEverything that does not belong on a slide

  • Calculation Handbook
    Notation, formulae, coefficients, derivations, solutions

    The full notation and unit-conversion tables · a formula sheet for all seven units · derivations of the four solids, the quarter-girth ratio, the spacing factor, the six sine cases and the m.a.i culmination · step-by-step spline evaluation · complete coefficient tables for the Bhutan volume and biomass equations and the published form factors · the Bhutan protocol reference · and worked solutions to the practice questions in every unit

How the three products fit together

The decks carry the concepts, the diagrams, the Bhutan procedures and the selected worked examples — everything that has to be seen from the back of the room. These notes carry the explanation you need to study on your own. The handbook carries the long derivations, the complete tables and the answers. All three use the same terminology, notation, units, equations and Bhutan-specific conventions.
