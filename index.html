Supplementary readings

Four documents to read beside the decks — three of them the working instruments of the Department of Forests and Park Services, so the equations and procedures taught here are the ones a graduate will actually be handed in the field.

  • Forest Resources Management Division · DoFPS · 2018

    Species-Specific Volume Equation — Pinus wallichiana

    Sixteen candidate models fitted for merchantable volume — on DBH, on basal area, on D²H and on BAH — compared by AIC and BIC, with a worked demonstration of the selected model and a frank statement of its limits. The full dataset is annexed.

    Unit VPDF · 48 pp · 2.5 MB

  • UWICER & FRMD · DoFPS · 2018

    Allometric Biomass Equations — 14 species and 2 general

    Biomass equations from the National Forest Inventory of Bhutan: two for each of fourteen species — one on basal area alone, one on basal area and height — plus a general conifer and a general broadleaf equation. Biomass in kilograms.

    Units V · VIPDF · 4 pp · 311 KB

  • Forest Monitoring & Information Division · DoFPS · 2024

    Forest Canopy Cover Assessment Guidelines

    The national procedure for assessing canopy cover — the instruments, the sampling design and the recording sheets. Read it against Unit III, which keeps crown closure, canopy cover and canopy closure apart as three different things.

    Unit IIIPDF · 23 pp · 1.9 MB

  • Mercker & Yang · UT Extension W 1117

    A Simple Guide to Common Forest Measurements

    A short plain-language pass over DBH, basal area, trees per unit area, fixed- and variable-radius plots, age, height, crown class and site index. Written in imperial units — inches, feet, acres — so convert before comparing it with the decks.

    Units I · II · IV · VIPDF · 7 pp · 243 KB

Running a deck

Each unit is one self-contained file. Open it, press F11 for full screen, and drive it from the keyboard — or, on a phone or tablet, tap the right and left thirds of the screen or swipe. Esc brings you back here from any slide.

  • →SpacePage Down

    Next slide

  • ←BackspacePage Up

    Previous slide

  • HomeEnd

    Jump to the first or last slide

  • EscC

    Leave the deck and come back to this page — from any slide, not just the last one

  • E

    Edit the slide text in place. Ctrl+S saves, Esc leaves edit mode

  • O?T

    Slide index, shortcuts and class timer — Unit II only

Edits stay on the machine that made them. Pressing E and saving writes to that browser's local storage only — it does not change the file and no one else sees it. To publish a correction for everyone, edit the HTML and push it.

Decks are laid out on a fixed 1920 × 1080 stage and scaled to fit, so they look the same on a laptop as on the projector. Anything narrower than about 900 px will letterbox heavily.
