Procedure read the whole of it before the first observation

  1. Establish the circular plot of 12.62 m radius from the supplied plot centre.
  2. Lay the 2.5 × 2.5 m grid from the pre-generated point layout. Do not choose your own points.
  3. Read 89 systematic + 11 additional points. The 11 come from the quadrant assigned to your group — the same quadrant in every plot.
  4. At each point: level, sight vertically, and record 1 if more than 50 % of the inner black circle is covered by canopy, 0 otherwise.
  5. Total the 1s in the field, and compute the plot summary before you leave the plot.
