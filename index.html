Supplementary readings

Six documents to read beside the decks — five of them the working instruments of the Department of Forests and Park Services, so the equations and procedures taught here are the ones a graduate will actually be handed in the field. The first two are the source of the national protocol this module measures by.

  • Second National Forest Inventory · DoFPS · 2023

    State of Forest Report — Volume I

    The inventory this module measures by. Over-bark DBH at 1.37 m, trees from 10 cm and saplings from 5 cm, three 12.62 m-radius plots to a cluster on a 4 × 4 km national grid, Open Foris Collect Mobile, and every estimate reported with a 95 % confidence interval. The 69.71 % forest cover figure used throughout the decks is this report's.

    All unitsPDF · 190 pp · 6.5 MB

  • Second National Forest Inventory · DoFPS · 2023

    State of Forest Carbon Report — Volume II

    Where the tree measurements end up: biomass and carbon by pool, by forest type and by dzongkhag, with the uncertainty carried through. Read it after Unit VI to see a plot measurement become a national figure — and to see why a 5 % bias in diameter is not a small thing.

    Units V · VI · VIIPDF · 127 pp · 11.2 MB

  • Forest Resources Management Division · DoFPS · 2018

    Species-Specific Volume Equation — Pinus wallichiana

    Sixteen candidate models fitted for merchantable volume — on DBH, on basal area, on D²H and on BAH — compared by AIC and BIC, with a worked demonstration of the selected model and a frank statement of its limits. Models 7 and 16, the published validation tree and the model’s domain are taught in Unit V.

    Unit VPDF · 48 pp · 2.5 MB

  • UWICER & FRMD · DoFPS · 2018

    Allometric Biomass Equations — 14 species and 2 general

    Biomass equations from the National Forest Inventory of Bhutan: two for each of fourteen species — one on basal area alone, one on basal area and height — plus a general conifer and a general broadleaf equation. Biomass in kilograms. Both routes, the selection hierarchy and a comparative exercise are taught in Unit VI.

    Units V · VIPDF · 4 pp · 311 KB

  • Forest Monitoring & Information Division · DoFPS · 2024

    Forest Canopy Cover Assessment Guidelines

    The national procedure for assessing canopy cover — the instruments, the sampling design and the recording sheets. It is the spine of Unit III Section C, which teaches it before the comparative methods and builds the field practical and data sheet on it.

    Unit IIIPDF · 23 pp · 1.9 MB

  • Mercker & Yang · UT Extension W 1117

    A Simple Guide to Common Forest Measurements

    A short plain-language pass over DBH, basal area, trees per unit area, fixed- and variable-radius plots, age, height, crown class and site index. Written in imperial units — inches, feet, acres — so convert before comparing it with the decks.

    Units I · II · IV · VIPDF · 7 pp · 243 KB

A reading document for each unit, written to be studied on your own before and after class, and one handbook holding the long derivations, the complete coefficient tables and the worked solutions. Same terminology, notation, units and Bhutan procedures as the decks.

Calculators and field sheets

Two working spreadsheets and three printable field sheets, built from the same equations and procedures the decks teach. The calculators check themselves against the published figures.

  • Excel · from FRMD, DoFPS 2018

    Merchantable volume calculator — Pinus wallichiana

    Type a DBH and a height; the sheet computes basal area, BAH, the restricted cubic spline term and the volume from Models 7 and 16, and warns when the tree falls outside the fitted range. Reproduces the study's own validation tree — 54.1 cm / 23.4 m → 2.237 m³ against a field-measured 2.109 m³.

    Unit VXLSX · 3 sheets

  • Excel · from UWICER & FRMD, DoFPS 2018

    Biomass calculator — 14 species and 2 general

    Pick a species from the list; the sheet looks up its coefficients, knots and calibration range and returns biomass in kilograms from DBH alone and from DBH and height, so the two routes can be compared tree by tree. Reproduces the Unit VI figures exactly.

    Units V · VIXLSX · 4 sheets

  • Field sheet · A4, one side

    Canopy cover assessment — the national procedure

    The 100-point tally grid, the plot summary and the forest / high-forest classification, adapted from Annexure 1 of the 2024 guidelines. Print it, take it out, fill it in with a pencil.

    Unit IIIprint A4

  • Field sheet · A4, one side

    Diameter, girth and bark

    One tree measured three ways, the bark conversion, a tree list with exception codes, and a quality-control block completed in the field. The reference height used is a field on the sheet, not an assumption.

    Unit IIprint A4

  • Field sheet · A4, one side

    Sectional volume of a felled tree

    Section-by-section scaling by Huber's formula, then the same tree through Model 16, with the three causes of disagreement — definition, model error and measurement error — separated on the sheet.

    Unit Vprint A4

Running a deck

Each unit is one self-contained file. Open it, press F11 for full screen, and drive it from the keyboard — or, on a phone or tablet, tap the right and left thirds of the screen or swipe. Esc brings you back here from any slide.

Edits stay on the machine that made them. Pressing E and saving writes to that browser's local storage only — it does not change the file and no one else sees it. To publish a correction for everyone, edit the HTML and push it.
